How to Play

Get four in a row.

Connect Four is a two-player game on a 7-column, 6-row board. Take turns dropping discs and be the first to line up four of your own.

Getting started

Open two browsers

Each player opens the Play page in their own browser, or one regular window and one private window.

Join the game

Click Join game. The first player to join is Red, the second is Yellow. Anyone else can watch.

Take turns

Red goes first. On your turn, click any column and your disc falls to the lowest empty spot.

Connect four

The first to line up four discs wins. If the board fills up with no winner, it's a draw.

Ways to win

Horizontal

Four side by side in a row.

Vertical

Four stacked in one column.

Diagonal

Four on a slant, either direction.

Tips

🎯
